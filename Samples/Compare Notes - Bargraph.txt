Notes about bargraph sizing:
Report whether any of these rules conflict with our existing model of the bargraph.
The height of the render area always includes appropriate space for the label and spine, even when they are not enabled.
When label is disabled, it also disables the spine.
The width of the spine plus hooks is 8px less than the INI width setting, with 4px of empty padding on either end.
The hooks are always 2px wide.
The spine is always 1px wide.
The hooks always start at the spine and end 6px away from the bar.
The length of the hooks is a function of the label fontsize, with the spine aligned to a point 2px above the vertical center of the font (not at the exact center).
The bar is always 25px thick.
Defining the height of the gauge render area is a matter of calculating the height of the label, adding a pad between the label and the bar, and adding 25px for the bar.
If scale numbers are turned on, it does not affect the height of anything else.  They are simply added on below wherever the bar is.
When in vertical orientation, all of the above rules apply, but X and Y are swapped (everything rotates 90 degrees counterclockwise).
Exception: scale values stay upright (horizontal glyphs). They still track tick locations. Label, spine, bar, and value rotate. RW-6.86-TERMX-Layout03-live. Style 2 uses the same exception.
