LED notes:
With label off, dash pins the top of the LED to the top of the screen, strictly honoring the 0 Y position.  The editor does not.
With label on, the dash shifts the LED downward to make room for the label.  The editor does not.
The dash leaves 8px of padding between the label and the Y position, and no padding between the label and the LED.  Net effect is that the LED shifts downwards by 8px + label font height when label is turned on.
